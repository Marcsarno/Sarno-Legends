# Sarno Legends

A family turn-based battle RPG: Marc and Yola fight zombies in the hospital and the South Florida neighborhood. It borrows the battles and hero-building of Raid: Shadow Legends and Marvel Strike Force, and the turn order, break and limit-break feel of Final Fantasy 7, 8 and 10. Painted 2D battle rooms, 3D animated characters.

**Status:** design only. Nothing is built yet.

## Docs

- `docs/COMBAT_DESIGN.md`: the combat design (draft 3, simulated). Start here.
- `docs/RESEARCH_NOTES.md`: what the research passes found, with sources, plus the critic's and verifier's review.
- `docs/ASSETS.md`: the art we can reuse from Marc's other projects, and the licence notes.
- `docs/ROADMAP.md`: what to focus on next, the milestones, and the open questions.
- `tools/balance-sim/`: the throwaway simulation behind the numbers in the combat design.
