All CC0 (public domain). ## Voices (`public/assets/voice/`)

Text-to-speech, generated by us with `tools/voice/generate.py` from `tools/voice/lines.json` (placeholder lines). Not
downloaded audio: the model reads our lines, and the output is ours to ship. The model files stay outside the repo.

| Source | Licence | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Kokoro-82M v1.0 by hexgrad (huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) | Apache-2.0 | every voice line; voices `am_michael` (Marc), `af_heart` (Yola), `am_adam` (the Shield Guard, pitched and processed), plus the audition samples in `voice/audition/` |
| kokoro-onnx by thewh1teagle (github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx), with its ONNX export of the model | MIT | runs Kokoro on the CPU in the generator (not shipped) |
