# Sarno Legends: agent notes

Read `README.md`, then `docs/COMBAT_DESIGN.md` (the current design), `docs/ASSETS.md` and `docs/RESEARCH_NOTES.md`. Keep them up to date when things change.

- **What it is:** a turn-based battle RPG (Raid / Marvel Strike Force / FF7-8-10 style) with Marc and Yola against zombies. Same story world as Marc's other two games: Yola in the hospital, Marc searching the neighborhood for Arianna. Painted 2D battle rooms, 3D characters.
- **Status:** design. Marc asked for design and planning only so far; don't start building until he says so.
- **Asset repos are read-only:** `Marcsarno/yola-zombie` (Yola, Marc as `male1.glb`, zombies, weapons, hospital and neighborhood kits; most of it on the `neighborhood-level` branch) and `Marcsarno/survival`. Copy what's needed into this repo. Never commit, push, branch or comment there.
- **Story:** Marc decides story beats. Names, dialogue and stats in the docs are placeholders until he approves them.
- **Licences:** Kevin Iglesias clips (`kev_` / `kevs_` prefixes) can never ship. Mixamo, Quaternius UAL and KayKit are fine. The `t_` clips are open: the yola-zombie docs call them Tripo's own clips but one catalog labels some as Mixamo, so check before relying on them. Record every downloaded asset and its licence.
- **Work rhythm:** get Marc something he can judge early, before long automated passes.
- **Git:** push to `github.com/Marcsarno/sarno-legends`.
