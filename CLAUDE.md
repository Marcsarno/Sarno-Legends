- **What it is:** a roguelike deckbuilder that copies Slay the Spire's proven rules and numbers with a slight spin (Marc: "copy something proven that works. And add a slight spin"): Marc and Yola share one deck and one HP bar, Marc's cards apply Vulnerable ("Staggered") and Yola's cash in, and the killing blow picks a finisher or death animation. Floor by floor through the hospital; weapon upgrades carry between runs. Copy mechanics, never Slay the Spire's card names, text or art. (It started as a Raid / FF-style turn-based RPG; `docs/COMBAT_DESIGN.md` is that design.) Same story world as Marc's other two games: Yola in the hospital, Marc searching the neighborhood for Arianna. Painted 2D battle rooms, 3D characters.
