# What Marc (the owner) has said, in order, and the ideas that came up (for the design-director audit)

Marc is a dad making games with AI help. Two earlier projects: "Sarno Survive" (Marc searches an abandoned South Florida neighborhood for his daughter Arianna, held in a house; "Stay there.") and the Yola zombie game (Yola, his wife, a nurse in a hospital zombie outbreak, floors 7 -> 5; real-time combat with finishers: the Shield Guard's arm then head come off; the acid lady; perfect dodges; great combat and death animations). Chapter 2 of the Yola game is Marc's neighbourhood with looters (human enemies with pistols) and survivors.

Marc's own words and decisions, in order:
1. New project: a family version of Raid: Shadow Legends / Marvel Strike Force / FF7-style turn-based game, using the zombie assets and the Sarno family.
2. Family: Marc, wife Yola, daughters Arianna and Lilah, in-laws Chris and Kayla and their kids Jax and Harper, other friends and family. "Most of the time the kids are at home; Yola and I would swap out teammates depending on the story." "We have great combat animations already… great death animations… using that in turn-based combat could be fun and look really cool." Arenas can be beautiful 2D painted rooms, no 3D environments, "gives us options to go anywhere." "Stick to the same story [hospital + neighborhood], just change the gameplay." "The easy thing in Blender for AI is weapons, props… New characters are very hard." "I'm already hitting a wall with my imagination."
3. Holy trinity matters; "having just one character may not be fun"; keep Marc and Yola together: Marc tank or damage, Yola heals/buffs/damage. Moves need names and power scores; enemies too. "Challenging enough to feel rewarding and not frustrating or stressful."
4. Liked the turn-based draft 3 (Marc tank/breaker, Yola healer/finisher, Batter Up duo, intents, break, Second Wind). Decided HP carries between fights.
5. THE PIVOT (his words): "I'm think nailed it.. rogue like.. slay the spire… can upgrade weapons that can carry forward for future runs. I think we go the card route. Two heroes. Their moves are animated… balance … the random chance of cards as you go floor to floor of the hospital. Some rooms sort of easy… depending on cards… but even the easy ones, you can want to conserve HP." "I love love love that idea."
6. Probably one shared deck. Nothing story-like left behind between runs, just currency or upgrades. "Family members could be rare cards… or even better.. when you add them.. they have their own card sets that get added for that mission."
7. Only the Shield Guard is a boss so far. Chapter 2 added Unity/Mixamo characters: 10 survivors, 6 looters with pistol/cover/reload, 3 zombified survivors, Adam who can turn, Sophie a civilian, James with shop-counter moves.
8. Now: "I think we're drifting and losing strong ideas and strong true game-developer-like thinking with the type of game we're going with."

Strong ideas raised along the way (some may have been lost):
- Marc creates the opening (break), Yola takes it (finisher/heal) — damage belongs to neither alone; the couple must work together.
- Finishers and death animations as THE payoff; killing blow picks the death animation.
- Intents + no-clock + low RNG = strategy without stress; losses feel like your choice.
- HP attrition: even easy rooms make you want to conserve HP (Marc's own line).
- Story structure: Yola's hospital and Marc's neighbourhood as parallel tracks that cut between each other and reunite; the reunion unlocks duo moves (raised before the card pivot; the card plan has both heroes together from the start).
- Weapons are the cheap asset -> weapon progression (bat -> nail bat -> wire bat) as the main growth.
- Painted 2D rooms over the existing 3D scenes.
- Family as call-ins / home team / card sets; kids at home.
- Draft-3 sim showed a careless player has a tense win and a good player a comfortable one.

Features that accumulated in the card plan: shared deck, colour-coded cards, energy 3 / hand 5, per-hero block with Yola's block on either hero, targeting rules, stagger bars that carry, break, duo rule (one Marc + one Yola card), Second Wind, downed/Help Up, healing cap + horde clock, Bitten/Infected status cards, two elites, Shield Guard, supplies (in-run money), items, trinkets, card upgrades, card rarity + pity, reward safety nets, scrap meta currency, weapon tiers that replace starters, mastery goals, infection levels, family card sets, auto-save, speed setting, landscape layout, card art from clip stills. Now also looters and zombified survivors.
