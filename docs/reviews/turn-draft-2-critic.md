1. CRITICAL - normal fights trivial, healer idle. Sim of 3 walkers: AV timeline Yola 92.6, Marc 100, walkers 117.6. Yola Scalpel 34 + Marc Bat 42 = 76 >= 75 kills one walker per round before it acts. ~3 walker attacks land all fight (~125 HP), ~6% party HP/round not 15%. Grit hits cap 5 in round 1. Yola never needs to heal. "One heal covers ~2 rounds" false: 15%/round = ~105/round, 2 rounds ~210, Patch Up heals 80 (~0.75 round). Fix: Patch Up ~25% max HP, and harder normal fights; or accept healing matters only in elite/boss and give Yola an attack role. Spreadsheet sim first.

"Phase change at 50% HP" conflicts with "arm off at first break". Fix: phase 2 at first break; head-off finisher only <30% HP; stagger ~18 or shield regen; tune HP for 8-10 rounds.
