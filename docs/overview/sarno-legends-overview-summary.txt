WHAT IT IS
Family roguelike deckbuilder in Three.js (browser, phone landscape first). Copies Slay the Spire's rules and numbers
with a slight spin; names, text and art are original. Marc and Yola (Marc Sarno's family, same story world as his games
yola-zombie and Sarno Survive) share ONE deck and ONE 80-HP bar and fight zombies floor by floor up a hospital.

THE SPIN
1. Two heroes, one deck, one HP bar: each card is Marc's (red) or Yola's (teal) and that hero animates it.
2. Marc staggers (Vulnerable, +50% damage), Yola cashes in (bonus damage vs Staggered). Soft split: both deal damage.
3. The kill is the show: the killing card picks the death animation; a Staggered kill plays a slow-mo finisher.
4. Yola's healing is the starter relic (Nurse's Kit: heal 6 after each fight).
5. Meta = weapons bought with scrap (earned win or lose): 9 weapons, each swaps a starter card + adds a passive.
